**The Proposition, re-derived.**
- Two jump arms have distinct targets, since edges from one source are distinct pairs.
- A continue arm and a jump to $O'\ne O^u$ differ at $\tau_0$ under the right-continuous trace.
- A self-loop followed by the forced continue arm (A) retraces $\gamma_{O^u}$.
- So D2-classes map onto D1-classes, and the map is one-to-one iff no reachable decision carries a self-loop.

**The Kretschmann sentence is accurate, including its conditions under (A).**
- **When the target is the source.** The target equals the source iff $r'=r_0$ (A6). A direct path never gives that. A through-throat path gives it iff $\varepsilon=2\Delta\ell(r_0)$. That is a condition on $r_0$ alone, so it applies at either turning type.
- **Why such a decision germ recurs.** Under (A), a decision other than at the root is reached along a segment. Every orbit after the root is entered at a turning point (#290 (T1)), so reaching a vertex along a segment needs a second turning point on the same orbit, and the orbit is bound. The germ therefore recurs one radial period later, and §8.2 item 3 removes the self-pair.
- **Non-recurring cases.** Plunging apastra and unbound periastra are entered only by the jump that lands on them.
- **The root** is scoped (#309 K1). At $r=10$, $\varepsilon=1.2014$ at $c=0.1$, against $2\Delta\ell=23.663$.
- **The (A) condition matters.** Without an arrival rule (C5's model), a jump-arrived, non-recurrent self-target can carry a decision, as #308 §6 notes. The clause states the condition, but the bold conclusion does not (N6).
