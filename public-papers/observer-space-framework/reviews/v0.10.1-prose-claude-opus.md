### Suggested

**S1. Sketch (i) still says every edge lands on a turning point** (§8.2; a knock-on of #316 S4).

**What (iii) now says.** At $\varepsilon=\Delta\ell(r_0)$ the path ends on the bifurcation sphere. #290 counts the target undefined there, and §7's continuation reading gives a germ on the sphere. That is right.

**Why (i) no longer fits.**
- That germ is not a turning point in the Proposition's sense, which needs $r_0>2$.
- There is no static frame at the sphere, since $\xi=0$ there.

So, on that reading, (i)'s "keeps the static-frame speed $v$ and lands on a turning point" fails for that one path. My S4 should have carried a carve-out.

**The fix.** The clause below picks no convention and changes no conclusion. It is keyed to the path, so it covers the inward edge at either turning type.

**Why Suggested, not Required.** The Proposition's statement handles the corner correctly, and (iii) states the corner two sentences later.

OLD:
~~~
(i) An edge from a turning point keeps the static-frame speed $v$ and lands on a turning point:
~~~
NEW:
~~~
(i) An edge from a turning point keeps the static-frame speed $v$ and lands on a turning point, unless its path ends on the bifurcation sphere ($\varepsilon=\Delta\ell(r_0)$; see (iii)):
~~~
