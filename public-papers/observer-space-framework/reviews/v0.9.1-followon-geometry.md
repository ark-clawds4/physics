## (1) §11, completed histories versus a cut: needs a scope (F1, F3)

What §9 supplies is the co-existence bullet: the EPP1 kernels define a path measure on completed histories; "its finite-cut marginals are consistent by construction, and the Ionescu-Tulcea theorem (Kallenberg 2021, Theorem 8.24) extends them". *Per-vertex weight* defines a finite cut as one that every maximal path, finite or infinite, meets exactly once, and gives the per-vertex weight $1/D(v)$ there. *Dead ends* keeps a terminated history in the cut, unconditioned. The cylinders of a finite cut partition the path space, so the marginal of each is $1/D(v)$. This is what #270 §2 meant. The completed-vs-cut agreement holds wherever the kernels exist.

That is under three conditions:
- finite stars, since EPP1's $1/|A|$ needs them and countable stars need a named exhaustion (§9 *Exhaustion*);
- dead ends kept, since §9's survivors-only alternative is a conditioning;
- the hard gate holding.

The third is where the sentence goes beyond §9. EPP1 applies only where the gate holds (§8.3), and when a Zeno history passes, the measure on completed histories depends on the Zeno-mass convention (C35):
- **(Z-ii):** the per-vertex product, with excised mass kept on ended histories. It agrees with the cut weights ($X$: $1/2$ both ways).
- **(Z-iii):** the conditioned measure gives $X$ $1$, against the per-vertex cut weight $1/2$.
- **(Z-i):** neither is defined at the root.

Also, §9 admits proper-time cuts as finite cuts only "once clause (2) holds".

So "the two give the same weights" is supported where no Zeno history passes, and under (Z-ii). Unscoped, it decides in effect that the completed-history measure is the unconditioned product, which is the open Zeno call. Whether (Z-iii) is compatible with the ruling is, per #270, a question the record leaves open. The second sentence ("Still open: finite graph paths …") is correct as it stands.

## (2) §11, Zeno item: inaccurate as worded (F2)

v0.9.1 §8.3 lists three readings: (Z-i), a state-level gate; (Z-ii), mass kept; and (Z-iii), conditioned. None of them is a count reading, so "a reading by counts at cuts drops out" refers to a reading the paper doesn't list. It is true, since the note's counting (Z-iv) is the only one (#270 §2), but it should say it is not one of §8.3's.

"The per-vertex readings of §8.3 stay open" has two problems:
- **Overstates:** it calls §8.3's readings per-vertex. Only (Z-ii) is per-vertex (EPP1's shares everywhere). (Z-i) leaves weights undefined above any Zeno history. (Z-iii) reads $R$ ahead and departs from the depth-1 share wherever a Zeno history passes (C35: $X$ $1$ against $1/2$). In the note, (Z-vii) is the same.
- **Understates:** read as "only the per-vertex ones stay open", it would drop (Z-i) and (Z-iii). #270 left both open, because whether the ruling excludes them is not a matter of math.

The fix names all three as open and states the departure as a fact, not a ruling.
